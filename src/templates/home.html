About AZ Games

AZ Games is the A-to-Z shelf of the Poki2 network. The idea is plain: if a browser game is worth playing, you should be able to reach it in one click — no launcher, no sign-up, no "download our app" wall between you and the first level.

Every game page shows the game itself first, then the things that are actually useful to know before you commit: which category it belongs to, what it plays like, and what else in the same shelf is worth a look. Games run in an embedded player, so nothing is installed on your machine.

AZ Games is supported by advertising, which is why you will see ad slots around the player. They never cover the game area, and the privacy policy explains exactly what is collected. If a game is broken, or a listing is wrong, the contact page is the fastest way to tell us.
